# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and keep discussion focused on the work. People of every background and experience level should be able to participate without harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, or unwanted personal attention.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Attack ideas with evidence; do not attack the person presenting them. Remember that server reports may contain sensitive operational details, and never pressure someone to publish information they have chosen to redact.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these expectations. Repeated or serious abuse may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

If a conduct problem cannot be handled in the public thread, contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities should use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting instead of a public issue.
